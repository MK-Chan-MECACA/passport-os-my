## Product and source map

- Platform: ESP32-C3, 8 MiB Flash, 240×320 screen, ESP-IDF 5.5.x, LVGL, no PSRAM.
- Entry point `main/main.cc`; app lifecycle and screen sleep `main/launcher/app_registry.cc`; power and settings `main/launcher/power.cc`, `settings.cc`.
- Home, phone setup, cards and weather: `main/apps/home/`, `main/launcher/wifi_setup.cc`, `wifi_picker.cc`. Setup page source `main/apps/home/phone_setup.html` (gzipped at build time); phone location page `web/phone-location.html`.
- Xiaozhi and its faces: `main/apps/xiaozhi/`. Double-pressing OK anywhere opens a conversation; the gesture is in `main/launcher/ok_gesture.h`.
- Voice tools: `main/device_control*`, `main/voice_intent*`. Lists, reminders and Pomodoro are in `main/apps/lists/`, `reminders/`, `pomodoro/`.
- Radio, Muse, AI Voice: `main/apps/radio/`, `muse/`, `voice/`. Radio presets `main/apps/radio/radio_presets.h`.
- Tech News (Xiaozhi tools `self.news.tech_hot` / `self.news.tech_latest`): `main/apps/technews/`.
- City catalogue: generated by `tools/generate_city_catalog.py` from GeoNames data in `third_party/geonames-my/`.
- Games, Stopwatch and the rest have their own directories under `main/apps/`. The names and order of the 12 launcher cards are defined in `app_registry.cc`.
- Fonts `main/fonts/` (Latin-only, regenerated by `tools/gen_fonts.sh`), shared UI `main/ui/`; Wi-Fi `components/esp-wifi-connect/`, hardware `components/bsp/`.

## Behaviour inherited from upstream rc.59

- rc.59 (2026-11-11): Xiaozhi sends a WebSocket PING before exiting to wake its receive task (exit wait about 0.98 s → 0.1 s) and does not redraw "offline" while exiting. Home does not start a due weather request within 4 s of opening, so passing through Home no longer stalls about 0.9 s on a TLS handshake. Measured over serial: Xiaozhi → Radio about 1.4 s → 0.5 s. Locking the CPU at full speed during switches was measured to make no difference and was reverted.
- rc.58: the AI Voice phone/computer entry copies the project's GitHub guide link; the Muse proxy fields are expanded by default; the setup hotspot name is 20 px instead of 14 px.
- Louder alerts (2026-11-10): Pomodoro and reminder alerts are a 2 kHz / 2.67 kHz two-tone, a pair of loud 120 ms beeps every 0.5 s, at a fixed 90% output (about −2 dB) regardless of master volume, including volume 0. Normal volume is restored afterwards. Key clicks are unchanged (muted at volume 0).
- After a reminder finishes (done, snoozed or timed out) the device returns to the interrupted app (except the launcher). Battery warnings at 10% and 5% each sound once; the 5% warning says the device will shut down soon, and any ringing alert is stopped before shutdown.
- AI Voice allocates its UDP buffers on entry and frees them on exit (about 4.3 KB less resident BSS, about 1.45 KB heap while running).
- The phone setup page is gzipped at build time. Its token is passed to the page script with `Set-Cookie: pt=`; the API still authenticates with the `t=` parameter.
- Re-entering Xiaozhi within 10 minutes reuses the last OTA result from NVS and connects straight to the WebSocket, saving one HTTPS request; one failed connection with it fetches OTA again.
- Xiaozhi waveform: 13 bars under the robot follow microphone level while listening and playback level while replying, scaled by master volume and coloured by theme. Short text keeps the large robot; text over two lines opens an eight-line reading area.
- Power savings: serial polling drops from 50 ms to 250 ms while a non-audio foreground app has the screen off; the Xiaozhi screen-off face updates every 1000 ms instead of 50 ms; offline standby rescans at most every 300 s. Button debounce stays at 20 ms × 3. Manual rescans are rate-limited to one per 15 s.
- Radio voice requests skip the state lookup when the local target is found. The nationwide list remains available after a voice request. Radio start-up prefill is 8 KiB (16 KiB buffer, 8 KiB refill on underrun).

## Verification status

- Test and build results for each MY edition step are recorded in MY-EDITION.md.
- Radio (2026-11-18): the live Malaysian directory and presets produced 109 distinct stream URLs in the host parser. All 51 built-in feeds yielded decodable audio on the host. The regression exercises selection and wraparound with 400 entries.
- **Not yet checked on a real device:** English UI layout on the physical screen, AAC playback with Wi-Fi + TLS active (decoder needs about 23 KiB with no PSRAM), the English setup pages on a real phone, Xiaozhi with an English agent from Malaysia.
- **Power and battery life have never been accepted:** there is no current-meter data. Fewer callbacks must not be reported as a percentage of power saved.
- Real-microphone use, multi-day recurring reminder reliability and the state of external cloud services are not covered by host regression tests. Serial-simulated buttons are not physical-button acceptance.
- Upstream rc.59 was built, verified (first heap block 78,760 B against a 73,728 B threshold) and flashed by the upstream author. Its hashes and logs are in the archived upstream status log.
